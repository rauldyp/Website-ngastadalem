## Brand & Style

The design system embodies architectural quiet luxury, austere restraint, and spatial permanence. Built for an elite architectural studio, the interface rejects gratuitous decoration and transient digital trends in favor of structural clarity, volumetric proportions, and profound material awareness. It serves institutional patrons, cultural curators, and discerning private clients seeking contextual resonance and tectonic mastery.

The visual style merges **Editorial Brutalism** and **Tactile Minimalism**. It captures the tangible essence of poured concrete, warm sand screed, native teak, and structural glass through strict proportional alignments, expansive negative space, and razor-sharp typographic tensions. Generous breathable canvas transitions rhythmically into deep subterranean blocks, invoking the spatial experience of stepping from an open, sunlit courtyard into an intimate basalt interior.

## Colors

The chromatic structure operates on dual emotional registers: **Warm Sand Light Canvas** for overarching narrative balance and **Obsidian Noir Dark Stage** for focused architectural showcases.

- **Primary (`#111213`)**: Warm Ink Black serves as the primary ground for typographic statements, borders, and tactile high-contrast anchors.
- **Secondary (`#9E7250`)**: Timber Teak introduces warmth, material tactility, and craftsmanship, reminiscent of seasoned joinery and structural louver elements.
- **Tertiary (`#2563EB`)**: Clean Architectural Blue provides targeted surgical precision for interactive focal points, dynamic badges, and technical hovers.
- **Neutral (`#F8F7F4`)**: Warm Sand White establishes an organic, non-glare mineral substrate, preventing digital sterility.

The system utilizes contextual dark staging (`#0C0D0E`) for deep focus modules, project monographs, and terminal footers, bounded by hairline graphite structural dividers (`#232527`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy creates tension between sculptural Swiss-inspired modern grotesk forms and technical drafting annotations.

- **Display & Headlines (`Hanken Grotesk`)**: Imparts structural weight, authoritative proportion, and tight tracking. Used for statement titles, overarching themes, and monumental numerical metrics.
- **Body & Prose (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers clear readability across extended narrative context, project briefs, and philosophy manifestos with open counters and balanced geometry.
- **Technical Annotations & Labels (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Serves as the architect's metadata voice. Applied with all-caps styling, prefixed slashes (e.g., `// 01 ARCHITECTURE`), and generous letter spacing to evoke blueprint specifications, coordinates, and cadastral references.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on a 12-column architectural grid with structural margins and uncompromising discipline. Whitespace functions as a tangible building material—negative space creates breathing room equivalent to lightwells and open atriums.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px)**: 12 columns with `2.5rem` gutters and `5rem` horizontal margins. Maximum content boundary caps at `1440px`. Sections alternate breathing room using `space-3xl` (`7rem`) vertical padding.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `3rem` margins. Complex dual-pane showcases collapse into dynamic stacked or offset pairs.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` outer canvas padding. Hero titles drop from `68px` to `40px` to maintain strict visual equilibrium without unforced hyphenation.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects synthetic dropshadows and blurred elevations, opting instead for **Tonal Tiering, Structural Insets, and Low-Contrast Architectural Outlines**.

Depth is derived from planar physical layering:
1. **Zero-Shadow Planes**: Components sit flush against the canvas. Division occurs through crisp, hairline `1px` stone rules (`#E5E2DC` on sand, `#232527` on dark).
2. **Structural Inset Frames**: Project showcases and tactile cards use internal padding (matted picture frame style) to push content inward rather than casting faux elevation outward.
3. **Glass Translucency**: The persistent top navigation applies a calibrated `backdrop-blur-md` with `85%` opacity over `#F8F7F4` (or `#0C0D0E` when over inverted stages), serving as clean sheet glass overlaid across passing structural scenery.

## Shapes

The shape grammar adheres to **Subtle Precision (`roundedness: 1`)**. Rounded treatments remain muted (`0.25rem` to `0.5rem`) to echo hand-finished stone edges, milled timber, and bevelled plate glass. 

- Interactive controls, buttons, and chips use a strict `0.25rem` radius or deliberate `0px` sharp corners on structural dividers.
- Visual media containers, portfolio vignettes, and interactive service showcases utilize `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`) to soften imagery without losing geometric definition.
- Deep stage featured cards adopt an inset framing profile of `1rem` (`rounded-2xl`) to evoke custom architectural joinery frames.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Links
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#111213` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.25rem` radius, with `12px` JetBrains Mono uppercase tracking. Subtle scale down to `0.98` on active press. In dark modules, the treatment inverses cleanly to pure white fill with black text.
- **Editorial Text Links**: Inline sans-serif or monospace accompanied by a diagonal vector arrow (`↗`). Hover triggers a smooth transform transition ($x: +2px, y: -2px$) and a delicate timber teak (`#9E7250`) underline offset.

### Interactive Service Split List
- Two-column layout: left column contains service categories spaced vertically with muted tones (`#9E9B94`); hover switches text to `#111213` with a timber teak underline rule.
- Right preview container holds an inset 16:10 framed preview, transitioning images via a 300ms cubic-bezier crossfade alongside synchronized monospace project data.

### Portfolio Showcase Cards
- Encapsulated within the Obsidian Noir canvas (`#0C0D0E`) with an inset container background of `#161718` and border `#232527`.
- Images utilize an overflow-hidden mask with subtle 1.03 scale expansion on pointer enter. Monospaced metadata tags anchor the bottom left (Project Name) and right (Year/Type) coordinates.

### Chips & Badges
- Strict technical tags with a `0.25rem` contour, hairline border, and `JetBrains Mono` 11px uppercase typography.
- Neutral light variant: `#FFFFFF` fill, `#E5E2DC` border, `#6B6964` text. In dark modules: `#161718` fill, `#232527` border, `#8E9094` text.
- Active states deploy a hairline architectural blue border (`#2563EB`) with tinted text.

### Form Inputs & Fields
- Minimalist baseline architecture: transparent fill with a solid bottom hairline rule (`#E5E2DC` light / `#232527` dark).
- Focus transition smoothly elevates the border color to Timber Teak (`#9E7250`) or Architectural Blue (`#2563EB`) with a zero-shadow state.
